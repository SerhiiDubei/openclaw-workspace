# Home IQ Lead Gen Copywriting System

## Overview

This skill provides a systematic approach to writing high-converting lead generation copy for home services marketplaces. It combines research-backed messaging patterns, psychological triggers, and conversion-optimized structure.

## Core Principles

### The ZIP-First Philosophy
- Minimize friction (ZIP code is the only required field)
- Maximize trust signals before asking for information
- Deliver comparison value (up to 3 quotes)
- Create urgency through local availability

### The 9-Block Architecture
Every lead-gen page follows this structure:

1. **Hero** — Promise + Badges + ZIP Form
2. **Icon Row** — 4 Proof Points
3. **Finished Projects** — Social Proof Gallery
4. **Project Type** — Segmentation Selector
5. **How It Works** — 3-Step Process
6. **Main Article** — Educational Content + Benefits
7. **Reviews** — Detailed Testimonials
8. **FAQ** — Comprehensive Q&A
9. **Footer CTA** — Final Conversion

## Research Methodology

### Competitor Analysis (15–25 pages per vertical)
Analyze these elements:
- Headline structures and emotional hooks
- Trust signals and credentialing language
- Pricing cues and range presentation
- "How it works" flow patterns
- FAQ topics and depth

### Customer Psychology by Vertical

**Flooring:**
- Trigger: Worn/dated appearance, cleaning frustration
- Desire: Transformation, durability, low maintenance
- Fear: Poor installation, hidden costs, disruption

**Siding:**
- Trigger: Damage, energy bills, curb appeal
- Desire: Protection, beauty, low maintenance
- Fear: Water damage, contractor quality, weather delays

**HVAC:**
- Trigger: Breakdowns, inefficiency, discomfort
- Desire: Reliability, savings, consistent comfort
- Fear: Oversizing, high bills, winter breakdowns

**Home Security:**
- Trigger: Crime anxiety, vulnerability, family protection
- Desire: Peace of mind, control, deterrence
- Fear: False alarms, complexity, monitoring costs

## Writing Guidelines

### Tone Variants

**Professional/Trustworthy**
- Focus: Credentials, process, risk reduction
- Language: Precise, reassuring, detailed
- Examples: "Licensed pros," "done right the first time"

**Friendly/Local**
- Focus: Neighborhood connection, personal service
- Language: Warm, accessible, community-oriented
- Examples: "Your local experts," "neighbors helping neighbors"

**Premium/Luxury**
- Focus: Quality, exclusivity, white-glove service
- Language: Refined, sophisticated, detail-oriented
- Examples: "Custom solutions," "premium materials"

**Budget/Value**
- Focus: Comparison, savings, smart shopping
- Language: Direct, practical, benefit-focused
- Examples: "Compare and save," "get the best price"

**Technical/Performance**
- Focus: Specs, efficiency, long-term value
- Language: Precise, data-driven, educational
- Examples: "SEER ratings," "load calculations"

### Review Writing Guidelines

**Structure each review:**
1. **Star rating + Headline** — Specific outcome
2. **Story context** — Situation before solution
3. **Process experience** — Working with contractor
4. **Specific results** — Quantified when possible
5. **Emotional payoff** — How they feel now

**Example length:** 100–150 words per review

### FAQ Depth Requirements

Each FAQ should be comprehensive:
- **What** — Clear definition/explanation
- **Why it matters** — Relevance to reader
- **Factors affecting** — Variables that change outcomes
- **Typical ranges** — Specific numbers when possible
- **Examples** — Real-world scenarios
- **Action items** — What to do with this information

**Target length:** 150–300 words per FAQ question

## Conversion Optimization

### Trust Badges (Always 3)
1. Free/no obligation
2. Licensed/credentialed
3. No obligation/guarantee

### Form Micro-Copy
- Header: Benefits, not action
- Label: Clear, lowercase
- Button: Action-oriented with arrow
- Sub-text: Risk reduction

### Pricing Presentation
- Always provide ranges (never exact prices)
- Include "depends on" factors
- Show value relative to alternatives
- Mention financing if available

## Output Format

For each vertical, produce:
1. README with research summary
2. Complete page copy (all 9 blocks)
3. 5 tone variants (when requested)
4. Comparison table
