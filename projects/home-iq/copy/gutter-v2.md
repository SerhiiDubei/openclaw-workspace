<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 1 Question + Answer -->
**How much do gutters cost to replace?**
Most homeowners pay $600–$2,400 for a full replacement. Price depends on linear footage, stories, material, and whether you add guards.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**How long does gutter installation take?**
Standard homes finish in one day. Larger or multi-story homes may need a second day.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**What material is best for gutters?**
Aluminum dominates the market — lightweight, rustproof, and affordable. Copper costs more but lasts 50+ years. Steel is stronger but can rust over time.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**Do I need gutter guards?**
Not mandatory, but recommended if you have overhanging trees. Guards cut cleaning frequency from 3–4 times a year to once a year or less.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**Can gutters be installed in winter?**
Yes, as long as temperatures stay above freezing. Pros avoid days with snow or ice on the roof for safety.
