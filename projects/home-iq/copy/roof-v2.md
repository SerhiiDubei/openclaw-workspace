<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 1 Question + Answer -->
**How much does a roof replacement cost?**
Most homeowners pay $5,500–$12,000. Asphalt shingles are the most affordable. Metal and tile cost more but last longer. Size, pitch, and number of stories all affect price.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**How long does roof replacement take?**
Standard asphalt jobs finish in 1–2 days. Complex roofs with steep pitches, multiple layers to remove, or custom materials can take 3 days.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**Will insurance cover my roof replacement?**
If damage came from a storm, hail, or fallen tree, your homeowners policy likely covers it. Normal wear and age are your responsibility. A contractor can help document damage for your claim.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**What roofing material lasts the longest?**
Slate and clay tile last 50–100 years. Metal roofs last 40–70 years. Asphalt shingles typically last 20–30 years. Material choice depends on budget, climate, and how long you plan to stay.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**How do I know if my roof needs replacing or just repair?**
If it's over 20 years old, has missing shingles, visible sagging, or leaks in multiple spots, replacement is usually the smarter investment. A free inspection gives you a straight answer.
