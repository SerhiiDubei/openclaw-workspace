<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 1 Question + Answer -->
**How much do plumbing repairs cost?**
Most repairs range $175–$450. Simple fixes like faucet replacement or drain clearing run on the lower end. Major jobs like repiping or sewer line replacement cost $2,000+. Emergency and after-hours service may add a premium.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**Do you offer same-day service?**
Yes. Most metro areas have plumbers available for same-day and emergency calls. Rural locations may take longer depending on contractor availability.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**Is there a service fee for estimates?**
Most contractors offer free estimates for planned work. Emergency diagnostic calls may carry a flat fee that gets applied to the repair cost if you proceed.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**Can a plumber fix a slab leak without breaking concrete?**
Sometimes. Epoxy lining and rerouting can avoid jackhammering in certain cases. A camera inspection helps determine the best approach.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**How do I know if I need a full repipe?**
Frequent leaks, low water pressure, discolored water, or pipes older than 50 years are red flags. A plumber can inspect and tell you whether spot repairs or full replacement makes sense.
