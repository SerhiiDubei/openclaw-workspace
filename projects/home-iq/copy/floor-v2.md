<!-- SECTION: FAQ -->
<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 1 Question + Answer -->
**How much does floor installation cost?**  
Most projects fall between $1,529 and $4,852 depending on material, square footage, and prep work. Hardwood and tile cost more than vinyl or laminate. Get a free quote for an exact number.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**How long does installation take?**  
Single rooms often finish in one day. Full-home projects typically take two days. Custom patterns or extensive subfloor repairs can add time.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**Can I stay in the house during installation?**  
Yes, though it's best to avoid the work area. Professionals section off rooms and clean as they go to minimize disruption.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**Do I need to move furniture?**  
Most contractors include furniture moving in their quote. Confirm during your estimate so there's no surprise.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**What flooring is best for pets and kids?**  
Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and tile are the most durable and scratch-resistant. Hardwood can work with a strong finish, but it requires more care.
