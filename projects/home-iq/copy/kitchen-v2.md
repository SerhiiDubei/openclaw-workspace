<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 1 Question + Answer -->
**How much does a kitchen remodel cost?**
Minor updates start around $14,000. Mid-range remodels with new cabinets, counters, and appliances run $25,000–$40,000. Luxury kitchens with custom everything can exceed $60,000.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**How long does a kitchen remodel take?**
A simple cabinet and counter swap takes 1–2 weeks. A full remodel with layout changes, electrical, and plumbing needs 4–6 weeks. Custom cabinets add lead time before work even starts.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**Can I use my kitchen during the remodel?**
For a few days, yes. But once demolition and plumbing work begin, the kitchen is offline. Most families set up a temporary cooking station elsewhere in the house.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**Do I need permits for a kitchen remodel?**
Yes, if you're moving walls, changing electrical, or relocating plumbing. Your contractor should handle permits as part of the project.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**What's the best return on investment for a kitchen?**
Cabinet refacing, new countertops, and updated appliances deliver the highest ROI without a full gut. A fresh backsplash and modern lighting add visual impact for less.
