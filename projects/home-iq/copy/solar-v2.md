**How much do solar panels cost?**
Most residential systems run $15,000–$35,000 before incentives. The 30% federal tax credit cuts that significantly. Financing, leases, and PPAs offer zero-down options.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**How long does solar installation take?**
Physical install takes 1–3 days. Permits and utility approvals add 2–8 weeks before work begins. Your installer manages the entire timeline.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**Does solar work when it's cloudy?**
Yes, just less efficiently. Panels still generate 10–25% on overcast days. Your annual production estimate accounts for local weather patterns.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**What happens if I sell my home?**
Owned systems transfer to the new owner and add value. Leased systems can be transferred, bought out, or moved — though moving panels is rarely cost-effective.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**Do I need battery storage?**
Not required, but recommended if you want backup power during outages or live where net metering isn't available. Batteries add $10,000–$20,000 but provide independence.
