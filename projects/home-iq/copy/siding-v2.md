<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 1 Question + Answer -->
**How much does siding replacement cost?**
Most homeowners pay $5,560–$17,710 depending on home size, material, and any structural repairs. Vinyl is most affordable. Fiber cement and wood cost more but last longer and look richer.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**How long does siding installation take?**
A standard single-story home takes 7–10 days. Two-story or larger homes need 10–14 days. Weather delays and structural repairs discovered during removal can extend the timeline.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**What's the best siding material?**
Vinyl wins on cost and maintenance. Fiber cement offers the look of wood with better durability. Wood is beautiful but needs regular painting or staining. Match material to your climate and budget.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**Does siding installation require permits?**
Most areas require a permit for full replacement. Your contractor should handle this. If you're just repairing a small section, a permit may not be needed.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**Can siding be installed in winter?**
Yes, though vinyl becomes brittle in freezing temperatures. Pros warm materials before cutting. Fiber cement and wood handle cold better. Expect slightly longer timelines in winter.
