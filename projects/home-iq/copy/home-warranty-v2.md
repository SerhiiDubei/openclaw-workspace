## Home Warranty Plans: What You Need to Know

<!-- BLOCK: Lead Paragraph -->
A home warranty isn't insurance — it's a service contract that covers the systems and appliances your homeowners policy excludes. When your AC dies in July or your oven quits before Thanksgiving, one call puts a licensed technician on the job. Plans typically run $300–$600 per year with a small service fee per claim. For most homeowners, a single major repair pays for the entire annual contract.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 1 Question + Answer -->
**How much does a home warranty cost?**
Most plans cost $300–$600 per year, paid monthly or annually. Service fees per claim range from $75–$125. Combo plans covering systems and appliances cost more than single-category plans.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**What's the difference between a home warranty and homeowners insurance?**
Insurance covers sudden damage from fires, storms, or theft. A warranty covers normal wear and tear on systems and appliances. They work together — insurance for disasters, warranty for breakdowns.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**Are pre-existing conditions covered?**
Most providers have a 30-day waiting period and exclude known defects. Inspect your home before signing up so you understand what's eligible.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**Can I choose my own repair company?**
Some plans let you pick any licensed contractor. Others require you to use their network. Check the terms before you commit if you have a preferred technician.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**Is a home warranty worth it for a new home?**
New builds come with builder warranties, but those expire. Appliances have manufacturer warranties too, but only for a few years. A home warranty extends coverage and fills the gaps.
