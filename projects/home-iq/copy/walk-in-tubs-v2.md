## Walk-In Tub Installation: What You Need to Know

<!-- BLOCK: Lead Paragraph -->
A walk-in tub replaces the daily risk of stepping over a high wall with a low threshold, a sealed door, and a built-in seat. Most installations cost $3,000–$10,000 and finish in 1–2 days. For seniors, people with limited mobility, or anyone who wants a safer bath, it's a practical upgrade that doesn't sacrifice comfort. Hydrotherapy jets, heated seating, and fast-fill faucets turn safety into relaxation.

**How much does a walk-in tub cost?**
Basic models start around $3,000. Units with hydrotherapy, heated seats, and premium finishes run $7,000–$10,000. Installation is usually included in the quote.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 2 Question + Answer -->
**How long does installation take?**
Most jobs finish in 1–2 days. If you need plumbing relocated, electrical added for jets, or wall modifications, it may take 3 days.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 3 Question + Answer -->
**Will a walk-in tub fit my existing bathroom?**
Most models fit standard 60-inch tub alcoves. If your space is smaller or unconventional, contractors can measure and recommend custom or compact options.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 4 Question + Answer -->
**Does Medicare or insurance cover walk-in tubs?**
Medicare does not cover them as they're considered home modifications, not medical equipment. Some Medicaid waiver programs and veterans benefits may help. Check with your specific plan.

<!-- BLOCK: FAQ 5 Question + Answer -->
**How do you fill and drain the tub?**
Fast-fill faucets and quick-drain systems solve the wait time. Most tubs fill in 4–6 minutes and drain in 2–3 minutes. Heated seats keep you warm while waiting.
