# Game Mechanics Catalog

## Quiz Formats

### Bar Quiz (Classic)
- Teams: 3-6 people per table
- Questions: displayed on screen or read aloud
- Answering: write on paper, collect after round
- Duration: 15-20 min per round
- Best for: 15-40 people, bars, pubs

### Blitz (Speed)
- One person, 10-20 questions rapid-fire
- Time limit: 30 sec per question
- Scoring: correct = +1, pass = 0, wrong = -1
- Best for: high energy, competitive individuals

### Circle
- Questions move around the group
- Each person answers one, then passes
- Best for: smaller groups (10-15), intimate setting

### Yes/No
- Binary answers only
- Fast pace, quick rounds
- Best for: warm-up, breaking ice

## Team Formats

### Team Battle
- Two teams compete head-to-head
- Questions alternate between teams
- Can steal after wrong answer

### 1vAll
- One person against the rest
- Special rules to balance (e.g., one person gets 2x points)

## Social Formats

### Donetki (Gradual Reveal)
- Hints revealed slowly (30 sec, 1 min, 2 min)
- Earlier correct answer = more points
- Best for: difficult questions, building tension

### Thematic Rounds
- All questions in round share theme
- Theme revealed at start of round
