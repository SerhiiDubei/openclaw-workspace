# Game Flow: Start / Mid / Late

## Start Phase (0-15 min)
**Goal**: Hook players, establish energy

**Characteristics**:
- Highest energy questions
- Simple rules, minimal explanation
- Quick wins (easy questions first)
- Social interaction, ice breaking

**What works**:
- Fast-paced formats (blitz, yes/no)
- Funny or surprising questions
- Team forming activities
- Clear stakes explained upfront

**What doesn't**:
- Complex rules
- Slow buildup
- Individual focus in team games

## Mid Phase (15-45 min)
**Goal**: Core gameplay, maintain engagement

**Characteristics**:
- Balanced difficulty
- Variety in question types
- Strategic decisions emerge
- Rivalries form

**What works**:
- Mix of formats
- Thematic rounds
- Opportunities for comebacks
- Moderate challenge

## Late Phase (45-60 min)
**Goal**: Climax, memorable finish

**Characteristics**:
- Highest stakes
- Difficult questions acceptable
- Final push / sudden death options
- Clear winner emerges

**What works**:
- Final showdown format
- Questions that could change leaderboard
- Tiebreaker ready
- Celebration of winners

## Transitions

**Start → Mid**: After everyone comfortable with rules
**Mid → Late**: 10-15 min before planned end

Energy curve should peak at start, sustain through mid, spike again at end.
