## Competitor Analysis: Bathroom Remodeling / Walk-in Shower Pre-landers

Conducted comprehensive competitor research for walk-in shower/bathroom remodeling lead generation market.

### Direct Competitors (National Chains)
- **Bath Fitter** — bathfitter.com — "1-Day Bathroom Remodeling", PermaFit™ (install OVER existing), "Save up to $500" seasonal offers, 2M+ customers, transferable lifetime warranty
- **Re-Bath** — rebath.com — full remodels, walk-in tubs, accessibility focus
- **Bath Planet** — bathplanet.com — acrylic systems, one-day install
- **Jacuzzi Bath Remodel** — bathwraps.com — Jacuzzi® brand leverage
- **KOHLER Showers** — kohlershowers.com — LuxStone collection, 140+ years brand
- **Five Star Bath Solutions** — локальні лендінги під міста, ZIP-first форми

### Lead Generation Platforms
- **Minyona** — $41-99/lead, 100% exclusive, TCPA compliant, appointment setting included
- **EverConnect** — exclusive leads, live phone transfers, CRM integration
- **Inquir** — $30-120/lead, real-time verification via Trestle API, TrustedForm
- **Dolead** — AI-powered filtering, 15+ years experience
- **Service Allies** — Facebook Ads focus, 20-60 leads/month, lead + market exclusivity

### Marketplace Platforms (Shared Leads)
- **Angi / HomeAdvisor** — $25-75/lead, shared 3-5 contractors
- **Houzz Pro / Thumbtack** — subscription models, shared leads

### Key Pre-lander Patterns Identified
- Hero: "Finally... Affordable Walk-In Showers Built To Last" + "Installed In As Little As 1 Day"
- Lead form: ZIP code → project type → timeline → contact info (4-step)
- Trust signals: Verified Purchase badge, Lifetime Warranty, "No Demolition"
- Social proof: Location-specific reviews with full names
- Financing: "Same As Cash" options to remove price objections

### Competitive Differentiation Opportunities
- Bath Fitter weakness: "tub over old tub" complaints, alignment issues
- Shared leads weakness: low conversion (race to bottom)
- KOHLER weakness: high price point, limited city coverage
- **ZIP-first capture** — industry standard for minimal friction
- **Advertorial format** — dominant in high-converting pages
